Four short guides

From shortlist to shipment

01 · Links

How to verify CSSBuy product links

For a Taobao product link, a Weidian seller listing, or a 1688 wholesale listing, record the original URL, listing ID, and seller name. When comparing two listings, match the model code and selected color before comparing prices.

Check current stock and seller return conditions. On a wholesale listing, confirm the minimum order quantity and whether the displayed price applies to your chosen variant.

02 · Fit

How to read a CSSBuy size chart

Measure an item you own that fits well. For a tee, compare flat chest width and body length. For a hoodie or jacket, add shoulder width and sleeve length. On pants, compare the flat waistband, inseam, and rise.

For sneaker size conversion, use the seller’s shoe size chart and check foot length in centimeters. For caps, confirm the adjustable circumference range. For jerseys, check chest width and whether customization affects returns.

03 · Inspection

What CSSBuy QC photos can show

Compare warehouse QC photos with the variant you ordered. Start with the color and size label, then look at seams, fasteners, and obvious damage. For shoes, compare both sides of the pair and ask for an insole measurement if fit is uncertain.

Ask about close-up photos or extra measurements while the item is still at the warehouse. If a detail looks wrong, check the seller’s return window and local return postage before approving shipment.

04 · Delivery

How to build a CSSBuy shipping estimate

Start with the item price, domestic shipping within China, and any service fees. For international shipping, compare parcel weight and dimensions because a route may charge by volumetric weight.

Check route restrictions for your item and destination. If you combine orders through parcel consolidation, compare the new packed dimensions and the cost of shipping separately. Account for exchange rates and applicable taxes or duties.

What to save in each shortlist row

Record the original seller URL, item ID, color and size, seller measurements, currency, last-checked date, QC status, and estimated packed weight. Keeping the date next to the listing makes it easier to spot which rows need a fresh stock or price check.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

A CSSBuy spreadsheet is a shopping shortlist that organizes product links by category. Use it to find candidates, then confirm the seller, variant, measurements, and current availability on the live listing.

What should I do if a CSSBuy product link is broken?

Try the original marketplace URL or search the marketplace using the product title and seller name. Before saving a replacement link, compare the listing ID, color, size chart, and seller details.

Can warehouse QC photos prove an item is authentic?

No. QC photos help you check visible details such as color, stitching, labels, and damage. If authenticity matters, use a source that can provide verifiable provenance.

How do I estimate shipping before ordering?

Compare routes using your destination, estimated packed weight, and parcel dimensions. Include domestic postage and any applicable service fees, currency conversion, taxes, or duties in your budget.
